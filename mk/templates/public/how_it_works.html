How Kola Market works

Orders, payments and delivery follow a recorded process.

  1. Choose a listing

    Browse active products from approved vendors. Check stock, delivery area and price before checkout.

  2. Place and fund an order

    Checkout reserves stock and creates a draft order. Fund it from your available balance. The amount moves into escrow.

  3. Receive the order

    The vendor accepts, packs and dispatches the order. Confirm delivery with the one-time code provided by the vendor.

  4. Escrow is released

    After delivery, the order finalizes when its delivery timer ends. Open a dispute before then if there is a problem.

Order protection

When are order funds released?

Order funds remain in escrow until delivery is confirmed and the finalization period ends, or an administrator rules on a dispute. Finalization releases the vendor share and recorded fees.
