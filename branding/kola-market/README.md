# Kola Market logo set

## Direction
The horizontal K-first lockup is the primary mark. Its central negative-space diamond is the simplified split-shell seam: the K stays recognizable at favicon scale, while the seam carries the kola-nut reference at larger sizes. The seal is reserved for escrow and trusted-vendor contexts.

## Palette
- Kola orange: `#D4813F`
- Ink: `#0D0D0D`
- Warm white: `#F2F2F2`

## Files
- `kola-horizontal.svg`: primary full-color lockup
- `kola-stacked.svg`: compact lockup
- `kola-monogram.svg`: transparent orange mark
- `kola-app-icon.svg`: dark app tile with orange monogram
- `kola-reversed.svg`: black-on-orange treatment
- `kola-horizontal-mono-black.svg`, `kola-horizontal-mono-white.svg`: single-color print/use variants
- `kola-seal.svg`: escrow / trusted-vendor seal
- `kola-icon-{16,32,180,512}.png`: app icon raster exports
- `kola-brand-sheet.svg`: visual contact sheet

The wordmark uses Inter with system sans-serif fallbacks. The icon paths are resolution-independent vectors. Keep the seal limited to contexts where its trust signal is meaningful.
